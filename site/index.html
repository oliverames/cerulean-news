About
Cerulean News looks for Vermont health care news and news about regional health insurers based in Vermont. About every 30 minutes it collects headlines from Vermont newspapers, broadcasters, and public radio, from a handful of national health policy outlets, and from Google News searches, then keeps the stories that match its Vermont health care and insurer terms. It never requests anything from the insurer’s own website.
Updates
Collected from Vermont news outlets and Google News. Refreshed about every 30 minutes; reload to load the latest published feed.
Archive
Insurer coverage is backfilled to Jan. 1, 2026 and kept indefinitely. Other stories are kept for three months. Insurer press releases and social posts are excluded.

Scored only for press coverage that names the insurer. See volume, sentiment, and themes over time.
