Scope
Everything here counts press coverage that names the insurer. Excluded: Vermont health care stories that never mention the insurer, the insurer’s own site, national association pages, recruitment listings, Facebook and short-video items, and stories about a different plan that merely shares part of the name.
Volume
Counts every story in that set, including any collected too recently to have been scored yet. Sentiment charts cover the scored subset, so the two can differ for the current month.
Themes
Drawn from the terms the matcher found in each story. One story usually carries several, so the theme counts do not sum to the story count.
Scale
Five points: positive, neutral to positive, neutral, neutral to negative, negative. Tone is judged toward the insurer, not toward the story overall.
Method
Scores are AI-generated. Treat them as a first pass, not a substitute for reading the clip.
